Manifesto

Communicating is, above all, a responsibility.

Never has so much been produced. Posts, campaigns, activations, reports. And never has it been so hard to be heard.

Brands don't suffer from a lack of content. They suffer from a lack of editing.

C²N was born in the newsroom. That is where you learn that clarity isn't added: it's edited. That what stays out defines what stays in.
